# Privacy Notice & Data Protection
*Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Built to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) · ⟦OWNER TO CONFIRM⟧*

## 1. Minimal Data Collection
At Eskort Creations, we collect strictly what is required to fulfil your order and deliver your parcel:
- **Contact Details**: Name, phone number (for OTP/order confirmation), email (for tax invoice).
- **Delivery Address**: Pincode, house/street address, city, state.
- **Payment Identifiers**: Transaction IDs processed via Razorpay. We do NOT store your credit card numbers, CVV, or bank credentials.

## 2. Zero Unsolicited Marketing
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your phone number or email to third-party ad networks or brokers.
- WhatsApp updates are transactional only (order confirmation, tracking, support) unless you explicitly opt in to our workshop announcements.

## 3. Data Retention & Erasure
- Order delivery data is retained for statutory taxation and GST audit compliance (6 years under Indian CGST Act).
- You may request erasure of your non-statutory personal data at any time by writing to our Grievance Officer at `eskortcreations@gmail.com`.

## 4. Security & Storage
All customer data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and stored in secure Mumbai-region databases with strict Row-Level Security (RLS).
