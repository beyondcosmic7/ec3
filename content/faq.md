# Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Where are Eskort Creations garments made?
All our pieces are designed, hand-patterned, and tailored at our workshop in Jaipur, Rajasthan, utilizing local textile artisans and natural dyes.

### 2. How do I know which size will fit me?
We publish exact finished garment chest, waist, and hip measurements in inches on every single product page. We recommend selecting a garment size that gives you 2 inches of ease above your body measurement for comfortable movement.
