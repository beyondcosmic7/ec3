# Textile & Fabric Care Guide
*Handcrafted Weaves · Jaipur Atelier*

## 1. Breathable Viscose Rayon
- **Washing**: Hand wash cold or gentle machine cycle in a laundry mesh bag.
- **Detergent**: Use mild, pH-neutral liquid detergent. Avoid harsh chlorine bleaches or brightening enzymes.
- **Drying**: Line dry in natural shade. Avoid direct harsh sunlight to preserve dye vibrance.
- **Ironing**: Steam iron or warm iron on the reverse side while slightly damp.

## 2. 100% Pure Combed Cotton
- **Washing**: First wash cold separately with a pinch of rock salt to set natural dye pigments.
- **Shrinkage**: Our fabrics are pre-shrunk before cutting, with less than 2% residual change after wash.
- **Drying**: Reshape gently while damp and flat dry or line dry in shade.

## 3. Banarasi Soft Silk & Woven Zari Suits
- **Cleaning**: Dry clean only to safeguard metallic Zari embroidery and delicate woven brocade motifs.
- **Storage**: Store wrapped in breathable unbleached muslin cloth with natural neem leaves or cedar balls. Avoid hanging heavy Anarkalis on wire hangers.
