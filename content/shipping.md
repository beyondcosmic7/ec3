# Shipping & Delivery Policy
*Last updated: 22 October 2026 · ⟦OWNER TO CONFIRM⟧*

## 1. Dispatch & Processing
All orders are tailored, inspected, and packed at our Jaipur workshop. Standard orders dispatch within 24–48 hours of order confirmation.

## 2. Shipping Rates
- **Prepaid Orders (UPI, Netbanking, Cards)**: Free standard shipping across India on orders above ₹999. Orders below ₹999 incur a flat ₹70 shipping charge.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available for serviceable pincodes with a flat ₹50 verification and handling fee, clearly disclosed before checkout selection.

## 3. Transit Timelines
- **Rajasthan & Delhi NCR**: 2–3 business days.
- **Major Metros (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad)**: 3–4 business days.
- **Rest of India**: 4–6 business days.
- **Remote / North-East / J&K**: 5–8 business days via India Post Speed Post or express air.

## 4. Tracking Your Order
Once your parcel is handed over to our courier partner (⟦NEEDED: courier choice⟧), a tracking link will be sent to your registered phone number via WhatsApp and email. You can also track using your order ID on our `/track` portal.

## 5. Non-Serviceable Locations
If your pincode is temporarily unserviceable for prepaid or COD deliveries, our support concierge will reach out directly on WhatsApp within 12 hours.
