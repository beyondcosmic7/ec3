# Returns & Exchange Policy
*Last updated: 22 October 2026 · ⟦OWNER TO CONFIRM⟧*

## 1. 7-Day Exchange Window
We want you to be completely comfortable in your garment. If the fit, bust ease, or silhouette is not right for you, you may request an exchange or return within **7 days of delivery**.

## 2. Hygiene & Condition Standards
To qualify for a reverse pickup and replacement:
- The garment must be unworn, unwashed, and undamaged.
- Original fabric tags and selvedge seal must remain intact.
- Folded cleanly in its original protective packaging.

## 3. How to Initiate
1. Message our Jaipur concierge on WhatsApp at [+91 99291 91655](https://wa.me/919929191655) with your Order ID and photo of the garment.
2. Our team will schedule a doorstep reverse pickup within 48 hours.

## 4. Refunds & Store Credit
- **Prepaid Orders**: Refund will be credited back to your original payment method (Bank/UPI/Card) within 5–7 business days of the returned parcel passing workshop inspection.
- **Cash on Delivery**: Refund issued via direct UPI transfer or bank NEFT upon verification. The COD convenience fee (₹50) is non-refundable.

## 5. Non-Returnable Items
- Custom-cut fabric by the metre (sheared specifically to your requested length).
- Altered or customized pieces.
