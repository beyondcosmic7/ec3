# Garment Sizing & Measurement Guide
*How We Measure Every Piece in Inches*

## 1. How to Measure Yourself
- **Bust**: Measure around the fullest part of your chest with a tape held parallel to the ground.
- **Waist**: Measure around your natural waistline, approximately 1 inch above the navel.
- **Hip**: Measure around the widest part of your hips and buttocks while standing with feet together.

## 3. Built-In Seam Allowance
Every tailored kurta and pant from Eskort Creations contains internal **1.5-inch let-out seam allowances** on both sides. This allows you or your local tailor to easily expand the garment if required.
